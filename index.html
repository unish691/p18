Web development is the process of creating and maintaining websites and web applications. It involves a combination of coding, design, and functionality to ensure an engaging and efficient user experience . Web development is typically divided into three main categories: front-end, back-end, and full-stack development. The front-end focuses on the visual aspects and user interaction using technologies like HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, while the back-end handles server-side operations, databases, and application logic using languages like Python, PHP, and Node.js. Full-stack development combines both front-end and back-end skills. With the increasing demand for online presence, web development plays a crucial role in businesses, e-commerce, social media, and various digital platforms. It continues to evolve with new frameworks, tools, and best practices, making it an exciting and dynamic field for developers.
